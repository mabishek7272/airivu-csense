Our Vision
03 / 23

Why Edge AI?

Cloud is not fast enough, not private enough, and not always there. eAIgleye brings intelligence directly to the place where it is needed — inside the building, on the site, at the edge — so decisions happen in milliseconds, and nothing sensitive ever has to leave.

01

No Cloud Dependency

Every model runs locally. Zero reliance on remote servers or third-party APIs.

02

Real-Time Intelligence

Detection, classification and alerting the moment an event happens — not seconds later.

03

Privacy First

Video and biometric data remain on-premise. Encryption by default, at rest and in motion.

04

Ultra-Low Latency

Sub-second inference at the source. No round-trip to a datacentre, no throttling.

05

Local Processing

Full pipeline — capture, inference, storage, retrieval — runs on the appliance itself.

06

Enterprise Security

Signed firmware, hardware root of trust, and role-based access across every layer.
